Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Welcome to Cue AI (Wingman). Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we process the following types of data:

  • User Content: Screenshots, images, and text conversations you upload or paste into the app to generate AI replies.
  • Profile Data: Preferences you set within the app (e.g., age range, texting style) to personalize the AI output.
  • Device and Usage Data: Basic analytics such as device type, operating system, and app crash reports to help us improve performance.
  • Purchase History: Information related to your subscriptions, handled securely by native app stores.

2. How We Handle Your Conversations

Because Cue AI analyzes your private messages, we enforce strict data handling protocols:

  • No AI Training: The third-party AI models we use via API (such as OpenAI and Google) are strictly configured not to use your screenshots or text inputs to train their public AI models.
  • Temporary Storage: Screenshots uploaded for analysis are securely transmitted, temporarily stored only as long as necessary to generate your reply, and are not permanently attached to your identity.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate Cue AI. These include:

  • OpenRouter / AI Providers: For processing text and images to generate replies.
  • Supabase: For secure, temporary cloud storage of uploaded screenshots during processing.
  • RevenueCat: To manage premium subscriptions and validate purchases.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements (for free-tier users).

4. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. All data transmitted between your device and our servers (or third-party providers) is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Children's Privacy

Cue AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it immediately.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
